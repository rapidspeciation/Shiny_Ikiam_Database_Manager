## How data move through the work

- Insectary staff keep a **notebook at the cages** for emergence and mating observations. The January 2025 meeting says formulas added to `Insectary_stocks` calculate the number of adults and first emergence date per clutch **after** notebook data have been transferred into `Insectary_data`. Staff were told to keep the notebook because it also gives an immediate view of expected emergence. This establishes a real delay and dependency between observation and spreadsheet summaries. [Meeting 79, 21 Jan 2025](https://docs.google.com/document/d/174whQczX1UUwCQimL7R372jwUarMmCt6ziUwwBYHxGk/edit)
- Eggs are tracked by clutch, with species, date laid, number of eggs, hatch date, and larval count. A May 2026 summary shows these fields for clutches 944, 946, and 949; the following meeting records clutch 944 reaching 14 pupae with 10 larvae still developing. A later experiment follows emerged males from that clutch into two feeding treatments and records deaths before sample collection. Thus a clutch can move through egg, larva, pupa, adult, treatment, death/preservation, and specimen stages on different days. [Meeting 122, 7 May 2026](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fBE-8AnIeE10sA-FhixfKe19KFwEpRUMazPzjysi6Q8/edit), [Meeting 123, 14 May 2026](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-RbQY1Z9gXK4bS1aoPv7F5LcHcwomOOODgt83nGswQM/edit), [Meeting 124, 28 May 2026](https://docs.google.com/document/d/173zYNuh-WdVxnKfaJda3Z72hCuvbgC4xFPVlEYKpOR8/edit)

In August, the status table listed mother/father IDs and start/end dates; it reported attempts with no observed mating, one female whose eggs were fungus contaminated and did not hatch, and ongoing attempts. ## Recurring recording and identity problems

- The meeting record repeatedly describes transcription from paper to the workbook. A September 2025 pheromone extraction had insectary ID, extraction date, CAM ID, and tube ID in a notebook, with transfer to the database assigned to another team member because the collector was busy. Another September note repeats that the preserved butterflies' data still needed entry. The August 2025 agenda names an “App that speeds up data entry into the database,” but its body gives **no description or delivery status**. The notes therefore support a faster entry workflow as a need, not a claim that this app existed or worked. [Meeting 104, 2 Sep 2025](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rf9H_TWHb1NILvjhKV10H7VwE4cH0Zwbb7do-CBYrKQ/edit), [Meeting 105, 9 Sep 2025](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1l1qXsQoaofhKnikP4ak8w_xt17XeATQbylNosH1uMME/edit), [Meeting 102, 12 Aug 2025](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JZ598VvBgYeYFLp_U6-VlQmST5u4oSyxJv7PJb43Y1U/edit)
- Identifier integrity has been a persistent concern: April 2023 notes counted 21 duplicate CAM IDs and discussed preventing simultaneous allocation from skipping or duplicating IDs. A July 2025 curation exercise matched CAM IDs across database rows, wing photos, and physical wing samples and found photos without database records. In July 2026, duplicated Brazil photos appeared in folders for other countries and the team returned to original CAM IDs to verify them. These are distinct issues, but all require a stable specimen identity and review of source records before linking media or samples. [Meeting 12, 6 Apr 2023](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TGZc0RQ_twa_lmUgKHnxzxZMC4ULueRcBOfm_a5c2V0/edit), [Meeting 100, 15 Jul 2025](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OWUtX_gnpjCAPfTT91yx39iDOw7wcMT_aJW40HAPInc/edit), [Meeting 130, 9 Jul 2026](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1X83SvdyMR_SK07HbIOK8dh7TyHJ510zkVxSY5IdvUrA/edit), [Meeting 131, 16 Jul 2026](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11PljVGK9bkbSSFgYCY_RhW_SEHLV_GrNkQjS9b9ItKg/edit)
- A 2024 meeting said some F1s could not be used for a mutation-rate family because parent specimens were not preserved, and directed a change to their `Research_purpose` in `Insectary_data`. The same meeting required individual photos with IDs and a reason in the database when preservation used ethanol as a fallback. These examples show why a row's research eligibility and preservation path cannot safely be inferred from a simple “alive/dead” status. [Meeting 62, 20 Jun 2024](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1CG608BEauiLsHIh_boCFhOvmqPVWcsEQrlV5ksBxnQ8/edit)

The October 2025 meeting emphasized hourly observation to establish whether mating had happened before explaining failed hatch, rather than relying on camera footage. A capture interface should preserve **observed events and uncertainty**, not derive fertilization or mating from egg presence alone. ## Implications for the proposed collection tool

For a first phone prototype aimed at replacing notebook entry, the meeting evidence supports four concrete workflows, in this order:

1. **Clutch rounds at the cage:** select a clutch, enter the observation date and egg/larva/pupa/adult counts or stage change, and show the latest count plus first emergence date. This reproduces the notebook's immediate view while providing the fields needed by `Insectary_stocks` and `Insectary_data`.
2. **Individual outcomes:** select an insectary ID, record emergence, death, and preservation as distinct dated observations, with optional CAM ID and sample/tube reference when appropriate. This preserves the difference between a live adult, a dead individual, and a preserved specimen.
3. **Cross attempts and egg outcomes:** link mother and father IDs, record start/end and observed courtship or mating, then attach clutch counts and hatch/preservation outcomes with reasons. Avoid inferring mating or fertilization from eggs alone.
4. **Review before spreadsheet transfer:** show untransferred entries and validate clutch, parent, CAM, and tube references before writing the three tables used by cross summaries. The phone must give cage workers a usable current view if it is to replace the notebook rather than merely create another transcription stage.

These are design inferences from the sources above, not features specified verbatim in a meeting. Pheromone extraction metadata (individual, treatment, extraction date, CAM ID, tube/vial) is a close next slice once the core individual and sample links are sound.

The notes do **not** specify a phone operating system, offline requirements, user permissions, exact field validation rules, a preferred sync mechanism, or whether the 2025 “app” was prototyped. Those decisions need the current workbook, actual field users, and existing code to settle. During rollout, verify that the phone reproduces the notebook's immediate cage view and reconciles cleanly to the workbook before retiring paper entry; the January 2025 meeting explicitly valued that local view.
