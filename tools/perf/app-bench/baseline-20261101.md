## Hot spots, ranked

1. **The lab's whole workbook kept in memory, and a heap allowed to grow to 8 GB** (lab only). LocalSheets keeps
   the seed's rows: the heap profile at stop has `normalizeSeedRow` (sheets.mjs:839) holding 593 MB plus 121 MB of
   its `structuredClone`, after a `JSON.parse` of the 356 MB snapshot at every start. With
   `--max-old-space-size=8192` V8 seldom collects, so the server sits at 3.4 GB idle and peaks at 4.1–4.3 GB
   (5.0 GB profiled). Production has none of this, but it is what fills this PC (the memory detective's
   "lean lab mode").
2. **Search runs on the main thread and scans every sheet.** `searchSheet` (search.mjs:66) is the busiest function
   of the whole run: 14.9 s self time on the main thread, ~150–500 ms per `/api/search`. Under 5 clients it is most
   of the 25.6 s main-thread CPU; the event loop is then up to 1 s late for everyone (p95 397 ms). It affects
   production too (the Buscador tab, `/api/ids`).
3. **A sync blocks the event loop for ~2 s** and takes 10 s: in the lab `LocalSheets.readSheet` clones each sheet
   (`structuredClone` 7.3 s of main-thread time over the run) and hashes it (`crypto` update 1.3 s), then
   `reconcile` (store.mjs:780) 2.3 s. The reconcile and hashing are the same in production.
4. **The rack reader enlarges each tube's crop in plain JS.** `enlarge` (rack-reader.mjs:219) is 6.1 s of a tools
   worker's 14.6 s: ~6 s per `rack_read_photos` call, first or again. zxing itself is ~2.6 s.
5. **Synchronous compression and per-worker copies.** `sendTagged` compresses big answers with
   `brotliCompressSync`/`gzipSync` on the main thread (`node:zlib processChunkSync` 2.0 s); the tabs' loads
   (Buscador 1.1 MB, Monitoreo 1.0 MB) are where the event loop is 1.1 s late. The checks worker holds its own copy
   of the sheets' rows (`load`, checks.mjs:159: 236 MB live) and each tools worker fetches and parses the maps'
   data files itself (host_plants first call 6 s; up to 3 tools workers).

Also seen: the Asistente tab takes 5.3 s and 801 requests (T3's page through the app's proxy), and keeps polling
(`/api/table/changes`, `/api/chat/proposals` every ~2 s). The identifier process was gone within 2 s of every stop
(no orphans).
