# Racks: photos → deposit proposal → deposit sheet

1. `rack_read_photos` with the photos (attachment names, 1 to 6 of the same rack). It merges them by tube ID and puts the rack at once into this chat's deposit proposal in «Cambios propuestos» (`deposit`: its link, and `ask`: what is still missing). Several racks in one chat: one deposit, one section and one Anexo1 sheet per rack; the same rack read again replaces its section and keeps its fields.
2. A1: the rack's printed code (the square beside a corner) places it. The team's racks have it beside A12, so the tool takes that as the default: `assumed`. Tell the person and ask them to confirm, looking from above. The photos allow only two wells for it (`code_options`, A12 or H1 on FK00274230); if they say the other, `rack_deposit` with that `code_beside` turns the rack. Tubes go into racks in any order: their IDs only check the corner when they happen to follow a sequence (`checked_by_ids`). No code read in any photo: ask for a photo where it shows, or the tube in A1 (`a1_tube`) or at another corner (`corner_tube`), and read again. Never guess A1.
3. Then ask, in one message, for what `ask` lists: responsable (the person signed in is assumed: confirm it), estantería, fila de rack, caja; the freezer is LBI062 unless they say otherwise. `missing` wells: another photo from another angle (send all photos again), the ID from the tube's side label, or whether the well is empty. Give the answers to `rack_deposit` (`racks: [{ rack, estanteria, fila_de_rack, caja, fill, empty }]`, `responsable`). The person can type the same in the sidebar; the grid and the rows follow either way.
4. Say what the deposit implies for the workbook: the tube-locations rows (new rows; tubes whose rack or well changes, old and new), `need_rows` (tubes no sheet of the workbook has: they need their rows from the person, never invented), `elsewhere` (tubes the sheet put on this rack that the photos do not show there), `duplicates`. Nothing is written until the person presses «Aplicar» in «Cambios propuestos». If `protected` is said, tell them the app's Google account cannot write that sheet: «Aplicar» will be refused until its owner allows it; the .xlsx still works.
5. The deposit sheet: the sidebar's «Descargar .xlsx» and «Guardar en Google Drive» (only on click). In the chat, `rack_report` gives the download link (`markdown`: put it in the reply as it is); with `drive: true` only when the person asks for it, a copy in the project's Drive folder «Depósitos LBI (racks)» (`drive.markdown`). `drive.error`: say Drive did not take it and that the download link still works.
6. `tubes_lookup` with the `reading` or tube IDs: the workbook's rows for each tube (CAM, species, tissue, medium).

Say how many tubes were read from the photos, and whether A1 is assumed (code beside A12), confirmed by the person, or checked by the tubes' IDs.

## Photo protocol (for the team, Spanish)

Voltea el rack y apóyalo con los fondos de los tubos hacia arriba. Toma unas 3 fotos desde unos 40 a 50 cm, con el zoom (2x) para que el rack llene casi toda la foto y todos los tubos salgan enfocados (de cerca, el teléfono solo enfoca el centro). Que se vea el código del rack (el cuadrado impreso en una esquina): con él se sabe dónde está A1. Cambia un poco el ángulo entre fotos (inclinando unos 10 a 15°, no más) para que el brillo de la luz caiga en tubos distintos; evita la luz directa del techo o del flash sobre el rack. Envía las fotos originales (no capturas de pantalla). Si el asistente dice que falta algún tubo, toma otra foto desde otro ángulo, dicta el número de la etiqueta lateral del tubo o escríbelo en la cuadrícula de «Cambios propuestos».
