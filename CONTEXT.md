# Ithomiini recording language

This glossary distinguishes biological events from changes to their recorded descriptions.

## Language

**Individual**:
One butterfly across its recorded collection, development, breeding, and preservation history. A mark or label helps identify it but is not necessarily unique across all historical records.
_Avoid_: Row, ID as a synonym for the animal

**Observation**:
A person's recorded account of a biological event or condition, with its observation date and any uncertainty. The time someone enters that account is a separate fact.
_Avoid_: Edit as a synonym for the observed event

**Recording correction**:
A change to a recorded description because an earlier value was mistaken or incomplete. Correcting a death date changes the account of the death rather than describing a second death.
_Avoid_: New observation when correcting the earlier account

**Record withdrawal**:
A declaration that a record was entered in error or should not be used, while retaining its provenance. It does not reverse a physical event such as a dissection or shipment.
_Avoid_: Erasing history, reversing the physical event

**Sample**:
A separately tracked physical material derived from an individual or another sample. A sample can have its own preservation, location, and handling history.
_Avoid_: Individual when referring to one tissue aliquot
